3. Evidence: add opt-in local usability sessions with duration, remote presses, assistance, and completion. Human and automated sessions must remain distinct. Create a facilitator protocol and an empty findings ledger; no fabricated interviews or usability claims.

The real integration plans journeys; it does not reserve transport or validate fares. Accessibility preferences are requests to the provider, not a guarantee of lift availability. No third-party endorsement is claimed.
